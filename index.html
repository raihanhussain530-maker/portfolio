About.

I’m Raihan, a third-year architecture student at the Manchester School of Architecture. Most of my projects start in Manchester and the north: canals, viaducts and brick warehouses, and the question of how heritage, nature and everyday community life can share the same ground.

I work across hand sketching, physical models, collage and digital modelling, and I’m interested in adaptive reuse, environmental design and architecture that gives something back to the people who live around it.
